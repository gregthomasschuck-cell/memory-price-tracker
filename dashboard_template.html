    Read with care. The DDR4 8Gb spot series is DDR4 1Gx8 3200MT/s chips; the contract series is the PC DRAM DDR4 8Gb benchmark. The NAND chart compares a TLC wafer spot with an MLC packaged-chip contract, so read the trend, not the gap. TrendForce sometimes revises the prior week's spot average; this page keeps each week's first print.
