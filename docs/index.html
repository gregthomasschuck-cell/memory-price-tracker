    Method. Every day the script records the 1,000-unit price break, stock and quoted lead time for every basket SKU at DigiKey and Mouser. The index chains the geometric mean of day-over-day price relatives for SKU/distributor pairs priced on both days, so parts that drop out or get added do not move it. Relatives outside 0.5x–2.0x are treated as data errors and excluded.

    Read with care. Distributor list prices lag contract and spot pricing; server DRAM, HBM and enterprise SSD price off contract and are not sold through distribution, so this basket covers the memory that does trade there. Samsung and SK hynix sell little through DigiKey and Mouser. Stock-outs can freeze a listed price.
